Our Story

Where the Mountains Meet the Table

Nestled in the charming mountain town of Oakhurst, just minutes from the south entrance of Yosemite National Park, Mountain Oaks Cafe has become a beloved destination for both locals and travelers seeking a genuine, soul-warming meal.

We believe in generous portions, fair prices, and food made with heart. From our legendary pecan French toast at sunrise to our award-worthy fish & chips at sunset, every dish is crafted with the warmth and care that mountain hospitality deserves.

Our staff are more than servers — they're storytellers, guides, and friends who make every visit feel like a homecoming.
